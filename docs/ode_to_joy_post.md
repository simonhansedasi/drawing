Day 1 of visualizing the unseen. An iconic theme from a famous symphony, rendered as a landscape.

The foreground is a repeated waveform of the melody, representing the individual notes as well as the overall pitch. 

The background maps the music's dynamics — marked soft for three straight entrances, then a sudden written crescendo to forte. The foreground carries the timbre: more instruments joining the melody without getting louder, until the whole orchestra bursts in at once.

Can you guess what piece of music this was?
